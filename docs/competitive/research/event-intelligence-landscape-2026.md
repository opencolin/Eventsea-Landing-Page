2. **Enterprise-only pricing** — structurally excludes companies running <20 events/year
3. **Portfolio-level not per-sponsor** — Lensmor analysis: *"stronger on event-level analytics than on individual attendee intelligence."* No per-sponsor benchmarking against category peers.
4. **GDPR/regulatory exposure** — data cooperative depends on organizer consent; European coverage limited
5. **No self-serve reporting** — G2 reviewer: *"Had to go to account team to set up reports."*
6. **No ISO 27001 certification publicly listed** (ExhibitorsData claims certification as differentiator)
7. **No free trial** — friction at evaluation stage

## 2. Direct competitor cluster

### 2.1 Lensmor — the price disruptor (HIGH relevance)

- **URL** [lensmor.com](https://www.lensmor.com/) · launched on Product Hunt **May 2026**
- **Database** 160,000+ trade shows (vs Vendelux's 250K)
- **Pricing** Free tier (2K credits) · $499/event one-time · $899/mo Growth (3 events) · custom enterprise
- **Angle** Exhibitor-side intelligence (which *companies* exhibit) vs Vendelux's attendee-side. AI Agent for autonomous discovery + outreach. Reverse discovery (input customer → find events). 95% email accuracy claim.
- **Funding** None disclosed; likely bootstrapped/pre-seed
- **Why it matters to Eventsea** Validates the mid-market gap — every Lensmor blog post explicitly attacks Vendelux's $20K floor. They are direct evidence of buyer demand for <$10K pricing.

### 2.2 ExhibitorsData / BizProspex / 10Times / WhoGoes — data vendors

- **Model** Pay-per-list, no platform
- **Pricing** $199–$6,000 per list
- **Differentiator** ExhibitorsData has 7-day bounce guarantee + ISO 27001; WhoGoes provides LinkedIn proof-of-attendance URL per record ($0.075/contact)
- **Relevance** Validates that "verified attendee data with SLA" is a recognized buying motion. Eventsea's V/P/U verdict system can position similarly without the per-list pricing friction.

### 2.3 Exhibitly — emerging European entrant

- **URL** [exhibitly.com](https://exhibitly.com)
- **Funding** €1.4M pre-seed (May 2026), New School VC + 100IN + Allusion Ventures, Ghent (Belgium)
- **Status** Currently organizer-side (AI personalization for event websites — 10–20x conversion claim). Exhibitor-facing product in development.
- **Customers** 114 events with Informa, Easyfairs, Hyve Group, Dubai World Trade Centre
- **Watch for** When their exhibitor product ships, becomes a third direct competitor (Vendelux/Lensmor/Exhibitly).

## 4. ABM/intent platforms — event overlap (NOT direct competitors)

None of the major ABM platforms offers a packaged "which events to sponsor?" engine. The actual sophisticated-sponsor workflow is: obtain attendee list → run through 6sense/Demandbase to score against ICP → use match rate to justify sponsorship → post-event CRM attribution. Bombora flips this model — publishers use it to *prove* audience quality to sponsors proactively.

| Platform | Event selection guidance | Attendee→ICP match | Post-event ROI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6sense** | Yes (intent-based overlap analysis) | Strong (6QA + persona targeting) | Strong (51% pipeline lift in Cvent + Jifflenow case study) |
| **Bombora** | Indirect (equips publishers) | Account-level only (no contact-level) | Strong (pre/post intent lift methodology) |
| **Demandbase** | Partial (ABM methodology) | Solid (Qualification Score) | Partial |
| **Terminus / DemandScience** | No | Partial (via Bombora) | Not documented |

6sense's internal threshold: **"If we can't get a 10x ROI, we're not doing it"** — 56x ROI claimed from their own ABM-events program ([Cvent case study](https://www.cvent.com/en/success-story/event/6sense)).

## 8. Buyer demand signals (cite in Show HN / sales decks)

From Forrester 2025 + IDC Oct 2025 + Vendelux/Bizzabo statistics:

- **86%** cannot accurately attribute ROI back to events (Bizzabo + Vendelux, cross-confirmed)
- **98%** of teams struggle to justify event spend to leadership (Vendelux)
- **90%** report events influence uncredited deals
- **70%** lack attendee list visibility before events
- **62%** cite ROI measurement as biggest budget defense barrier
- **Only 22%** have full event impact visibility to revenue leaders
- **Only 13%** rate event selection as fully data-driven
- **55%** begin pre-event outreach <4 weeks before event
- **Only 20%** of enterprises have fully integrated event platforms into the broader marketing stack (Forrester)
- **28%** of largest organizations have deployed 6+ event tech platforms — tool sprawl is real
- **Two-thirds** of event teams have flat or down budgets second year running (Forrester 2025)
- **58%** expect event budgets to increase in 2026 (IDC)
- **91%** say "independent, analyst-led content is critical to event success" (IDC)

## 9. White space — what Eventsea can defensibly own

Cross-verified across all 9 research streams, the following capabilities do NOT exist as packaged products in any competitor:

1. **Per-sponsor scoring with category benchmarking** — "Here's how your sponsorship ROI compares to 5 comparable sponsors at this event." No competitor offers this.
2. **Pre-event attendee verification with documented accuracy SLA** — Vendelux's "Predicted" tier is explicitly unguaranteed. ExhibitorsData is reactive (list-based). Lensmor is predictive but unverified. Eventsea's V/P/U system + bounce-replacement guarantee would be differentiated.
3. **Calendar audit as a product** — "We'll analyze your annual event calendar, surface budget waste, and recommend reallocation." No tool ships this systematically; only Vendelux "competitor show-tracking" is adjacent.
4. **Sub-$10K pricing tier with the same workflow shape** — Lensmor partially fills $499–$899/mo, but lacks portfolio-level analytics. A $3K–$8K tier with portfolio attribution is unserved.
5. **ICP matching at the organizer-side (sponsor→audience fit)** — SponsorIQ does this for sports/entertainment; nobody does it for B2B tech.

## 10. What NOT to compete on head-on

1. **250K event database** — Vendelux has 7 years of historical data + 100M+ attendee signals. Don't replicate; partner with or scrape narrower.
2. **Pre-event meeting booking + managed services** — Vendelux's ChatVDX + human Meetings team is a services moat, not just software. Hard to match without a 20-person ops org.
3. **Fortune 500 procurement** — PayPal/MongoDB/T-Mobile spend $125K/year. Different sales motion, 12+ month cycles, RFP/SOC2/MSA overhead. Not Eventsea's wedge.
4. **"Event Intelligence" SEO category** — Vendelux owns the term, ranks #1 for it, defines the Wikipedia-equivalent thought-leadership content. Compete on a different term (we proposed "calendar audit" + "per-attendee verification" + "BuilderShip-style screening").
